PrestigeSF

A receipt you can prove.

The leaf

Signed with ML-DSA-65. Checked by recomputing it.

The field

A sparse Merkle tree. The set of receipts, not their order.

The proof

Six steps. Then one line.

  1. 01 The key id is live.
  2. 02 The payload is SHA-256.
  3. 03 The leaf enters the sparse Merkle tree.
  4. 04 The chain binds to the tip before it.
  5. 05 ML-DSA-65 signs that leaf.
  6. 06 One JSONL line. Alter it and the next check says tampered.
